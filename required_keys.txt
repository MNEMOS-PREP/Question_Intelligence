At milestone M4 — where this module currently stands — **no code path calls a
model at all.** Selection, the three item models, the cold start, the provisional
theta and the whole 500-student simulation are arithmetic over floats. The
entire test suite runs with no key, no network and no database, which is what
makes the mis-specification grid a development instrument rather than a nightly
job.

A key becomes necessary at M6 (generation and the G1-G5 gates), and the
degradation is the platform's usual one: without it, generation is unavailable
and /v1/deps says so, while the bank, selection and calibration are unaffected.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A NOTE ON WHICH TIER
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Master plan 47.1 routing rule 1 forbids the cheap tier for anything whose
  output is scored. Generation output IS scored - a generated item's reference
  answer becomes ground truth for every student who sees it - so G3 in
  particular runs on a frontier model or the gate is not worth having.

  Budget: 11 puts batch generation plus five gates at roughly 2.10 rupees per
  item. 3,000 verified items is about 6,300 rupees spread over a year. Do not
  economise on the gates to save it; an unverified item poisons the ground
  truth for everyone who answers it.
